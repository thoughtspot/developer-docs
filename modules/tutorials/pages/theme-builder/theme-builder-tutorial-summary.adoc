:page-description: Wrap-up and next steps after styling embedded ThoughtSpot components with Theme Builder.

Whatever you theme next, the loop stays the same:

. Write your brand as *text with hex values*, not as pictures.
. Generate the theme in *AI mode*, with one intent per prompt.
. Lock in the details in *Manual mode*, knowing that manual settings win over AI mode.
. *Export the JSON* and commit it.
. Apply the theme where it belongs: xref:css-customization.adoc[`customCSS`] for the embed, xref:css-customization.adoc[`customCSSUrl`] for shared themes and fonts, and the xref:customize-style-api.adoc[style customization REST APIs] for instance-level branding.
. Prefer *CSS variables* over selectors, and recheck any selector you had to write after every ThoughtSpot release.
