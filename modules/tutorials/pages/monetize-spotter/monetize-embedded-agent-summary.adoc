:page-description: Wrap-up and next steps after monetizing an embedded Spotter agent with usage limits and paid upgrades.

Whatever plan or pack you add next, the loop stays the same:

. Make each plan a group for access, and give each host their own pool for queries.
. Embed Spotter, brand it as your own agent, and sign hosts in from your backend.
. Turn on the counter in Spotter, show the balance in your portal, and open a modal when the host nears or reaches their limit.
. Sell plans and packs through Stripe, and update pools only from the verified webhook.
. Refresh the embed after every pool change, and reset pools each billing cycle.
